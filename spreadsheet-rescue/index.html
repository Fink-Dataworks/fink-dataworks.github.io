The problem

A tidy sheet can still contain the wrong answer.

A maintenance storeroom needs to distinguish similar bearings, filters and conveyor parts. It also needs to know whether a count means individual pieces, boxes, metres or litres.

This fictional snapshot takes its setting from industrial maintenance and conveyor equipment. It represents no real operation.

01 / PRESERVE

Keep the original export

Every source row has a stable ID. Original text, uncertain entries and repeated records remain available for comparison.

02 / NORMALIZE

Apply only supported corrections

Standardize whitespace, case, explicit unit synonyms and numeric types. Preserve meaningful SKU suffixes and leading zeros.

03 / REVIEW

Ask for evidence where it matters

Route ambiguous records to a review queue with a clear reason and next action. Record corrections and classification decisions in a 305-entry audit log.
